# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Lahore Tech Events is built for a broad and welcoming technology community. We are committed to providing a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in the project, submits an event, attends a listed event, or uses the platform.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give feedback about ideas and implementation rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without permission.
- Keep event information accurate and avoid deceptive or harmful submissions.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and raise disagreements calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, doxxing, hateful content, deliberate disruption, spam, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

The same standards apply to project repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, community discussions, event submissions, and direct project communication.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through [salmanahmad.tech](https://salmanahmad.tech) with the subject line `Code of Conduct report`. Include what happened, where it happened, and any supporting context that can be shared safely.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, reject event submissions, or take other action they consider appropriate. The response will be proportionate to the behavior and focused on protecting the community.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

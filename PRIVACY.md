# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 21, 2026**

Lahore Tech Events is a community-driven event discovery platform for Lahore's technology ecosystem. This policy explains what information the platform handles, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

This document is written for the current product implementation and may be updated as the product grows. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may handle:

- **Account information:** email address, name, avatar, authentication details, and account timestamps when you create or use an account.
- **Event information:** event title, description, category, schedule, venue, locality, price, tags, image URL, registration URL, and organizer account reference when you submit an event.
- **Profile information:** the name, avatar, and public profile details associated with an organizer account.
- **Support information:** information you choose to provide when contacting the maintainer for support, feedback, moderation questions, or security reports.
- **Technical information:** information required to authenticate sessions, secure the platform, deliver pages, and diagnose errors. Hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers may process standard technical data as part of operating the service.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, payment card information, passwords, or private information about another person in an event description or support request.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Create and secure user accounts.
- Let organizers submit and manage event listings.
- Review, approve, reject, or update event submissions.
- Display approved public events and relevant organizer information.
- Provide event pages, search, filters, sitemap, and related discovery features.
- Respond to support and security reports.
- Protect the platform, investigate abuse, and enforce the Terms of Use.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the product.

We do not sell personal information. The platform is not currently designed as an advertising or behavioral-tracking service.

## Public information

Approved event information is intended to be public. This can include the event title, description, venue, date, category, price, image, registration link, and the organizer name or profile information displayed on the event page.

Do not submit information that you do not have permission to publish. If a public listing contains personal information that should not be visible, contact support promptly.

## Service providers

The platform uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase for authentication and database services, hosting providers for application delivery, and external image or registration services when an event links to them. These providers process information according to their own policies and the configuration of the deployed application.

When you follow an event's external registration link, you leave Lahore Tech Events. The destination website controls its own privacy practices, forms, cookies, and data collection.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain account and event information for as long as it is needed to operate the platform, preserve moderation history, address disputes, or meet legitimate operational and legal needs. Approved event listings may remain available as part of the community directory unless they are removed or archived.

You may request correction or deletion of your account or submitted information by contacting the maintainer through [salmanahmad.tech](https://salmanahmad.tech). Some information may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the platform. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately according to [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Children

The platform is intended for a general technology community and is not knowingly directed at children. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are not legally permitted to do so in your location.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the product, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published in this file with a revised date. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about a public listing, contact the maintainer through [salmanahmad.tech](https://salmanahmad.tech).
